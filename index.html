    Button dabaiye aur boliye

    This is the real app screen. Tap a request to try it.

    Safe for people who trust easily

    Older adults are the first target of phone fraud, so Kahiye is built to never act on its own.

    The last tap is always theirs

    Kahiye opens the call, the message, the cab or the cart. It never presses Call, Send, Confirm or Pay itself.

    It recognises scams

    Mention an OTP, a “KYC update” or prize money, and Kahiye says it is very likely a fraud and asks them to call family. Nothing opens.

    Real links, never guesses

    Shopping links come only from the brand’s verified website. Family phone numbers stay with whoever set the phone up and are never sent to the AI.

    How it works

    Each request is one round trip, usually a few seconds.

    1. They speak

      One tap on the yellow button. Android turns their Hinglish into text.

    2. An AI agent decides

      It works out what they want and picks one of ten actions, from playing a video to booking a cab. It never writes a link itself.

    3. Kahiye acts, out loud

      It builds the link from verified sources, says in Hindi what it is doing, then opens the right app.

    Why it exists

    My mother asked a voice assistant for the Mochi website to buy sandals. It gave her a description instead of a link.

    My parents don’t want to learn new apps. They want to say what they need and have it happen. Kahiye is built for them first.

    It runs on Android and is in private testing with my family. It is not on the Play Store yet.
