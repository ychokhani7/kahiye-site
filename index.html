Kahiye

Kahiye, kya karna hai?

A one-button voice helper for parents who find phones confusing. They say what they need in Hindi, English or both. Kahiye says what it is doing, then opens the right app.

“Paneer butter masala ki recipe dikhao”

Kahiye: Recipe ka video chala rahi hoon. Opens YouTube and plays it.

Press the button to hear another request.

What your parents can ask for

No menus, no typing, no searching. One button, their own words. Kahiye finds the real link itself, so it never makes one up.

They sayKahiye does
  • Mochi pe platform heel sandal dikhao

    Finds Mochi’s official website and shows platform heels from it. Lookalike shopping sites are skipped.

  • Paneer butter masala ki recipe dikhao

    Plays a recipe video straight in the YouTube app.

  • Ghar jaana hai, cab bulao

    Opens Uber and Rapido side by side with home as the drop, so they can compare fares and book the cheaper one.

  • Doodh mangwana hai

    Shows Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart with milk already searched.

  • Beta ko phone lagao

    Opens the dialer with the number ready. They press the green button.

  • Roz subah 8 baje BP ki dawai yaad dilana

    Sets a daily alarm in the phone’s Clock app.

  • Ye ghar ki location hai

    Remembers where home is, on the phone itself, for every future “ghar jaana hai”.

Built to be safe for people who trust easily

Older adults are the first target of phone fraud. Kahiye is designed around that.

The last tap is always theirs

Kahiye opens the call, the message, the cab or the cart. It never presses Call, Send, Confirm or Pay on its own.

It warns about scams

If someone is asking for an OTP, a KYC update or prize money, Kahiye says it is very likely a fraud and tells them to call family.

Real links, not guesses

Shopping links come from the brand’s verified website. Phone numbers stay with the family who set it up, never with the AI.

Why it exists

It started when my mother asked a voice assistant for the Mochi website to buy sandals, and got a description instead of a link. My parents don’t want to learn new apps. They want to say what they need and have it happen.

Kahiye is in private testing with my family on Android. It is not on the Play Store yet.
